Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Muuvlink collects, how it is used and how it is protected. It is the English version of our Turkish privacy notice. Muuvlink is for people aged 18 and over.

1. Who we are

SALT KREATİF REKLAM TİC. LTD. ŞTİ. ("we") is the data controller for Muuvlink.

Çınarlı Mahallesi 1572 Sokak No:33, 35170 Konak, İzmir, Türkiye
Contact: the contact form or info@saltajans.com

We process personal data under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us: your name, email address, password and, if you provide one, phone number at registration; the photo and bio you add to your profile.

Platform usage: events you create or join, team memberships and badges.

Location: used only when you allow it, and only to list nearby events. It is not permanently stored on our servers. Workout start locations are covered separately in section 5.

Technical data: session information, advertising measurement cookies (Meta pixel) and cookie-free visit statistics.

3. How we use it

We use this information to manage your account and session; to provide the platform (creating events, managing teams, finding sports partners) and personalise event suggestions; to send notifications and reminder emails; to keep the platform secure; to measure the results of our own advertising and decide who sees it; to count page visits and which campaign they came from; and to meet legal obligations.

Your data is not sold and is not used to advertise other companies' products.

Legal bases under KVKK: performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c); legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç); legitimate interest, including advertising measurement and cookie-free statistics (Art. 5/2-f); and explicit consent where required (Art. 5/1 and Art. 6).

4. Third-party services

We use trusted providers for server hosting, storage, email and push notifications. They receive only the minimum data needed to provide the service and may not use it for their own purposes. We may also share data with authorities when the law requires it.

Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. (advertising measurement): through the Meta pixel on the website and mobile app, the pages viewed and the steps of registering and joining events or teams; at those moments, SHA-256 hashed email address, phone number and name, IP address and browser information. This data is processed outside Turkey. Meta uses it to measure advertising results and decide who sees ads.

Umami Software, Inc. (Umami Cloud, data stored in the European Union): cookie-free visit statistics on the website: pages viewed, referring source, browser and device type, and country derived from IP address. No cookie is written and IP addresses are not stored.

Threshold (our internal activity dashboard): the full name of signed-in members, the page being viewed and activity events, so we can see how the service is used. Email addresses, phone numbers and message contents are not included.

5. Watch and sports service data

This feature is being rolled out. It applies only if you connect a watch or sports service to Muuvlink: Polar, Garmin, Wahoo, Suunto, COROS or Strava. Connecting is optional, and Muuvlink works fully without it.

Consent. Before anything is read we ask for your separate, explicit consent, distinct from registration. We record when you gave it. Heart rate is health data, which KVKK treats as a special category.

What we read. Summaries of your completed workouts: sport, start time, start location (not for Wahoo), duration, distance, elevation and average heart rate.

Why. To confirm that you attended a group event you joined, and to show you your own workout history.

Who can see it.

  • By default, only you.
  • If you turn it on for a specific workout, your teammates can see that workout. Sharing is off by default and applies only to the workout you choose.
  • Your heart rate is never shown to other members.
  • Data from Strava and Wahoo is only ever shown to you.

Who processes it. Only Güzel Hosting (application server, Türkiye) and Supabase (database, Ireland). By giving consent you agree to this storage in Ireland.

Watch and sports service data is never sold, never used for advertising, never sent to Meta or to analytics services, never shared with any other third party and never used to train AI models.

The brand's own policy. On the brand's side, its own privacy policy applies; for Garmin, see garmin.com/privacy/connect.

Wahoo. We do not store location data from Wahoo. Muuvlink only reads your workout summaries and does not send your workout data to Wahoo. Wahoo may use data sent into the Wahoo platform through this connection for any business purpose, internal or external, including modifying, copying, analysing and using it in any fashion for any purpose in accordance with Wahoo's Privacy Policy.

Withdrawing. You can disconnect a service and withdraw consent at any time in Settings. We then delete the workout data we imported from it, any attendance confirmation based on that data, and the stored access token. Deleting your account has the same effect.

6. Security

Data is transmitted with industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Our server infrastructure receives regular security updates.

7. Retention

We keep your personal data while your account is active and afterwards for the periods required by law. If you delete your account, your data is anonymised or permanently deleted within 30 days.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict its processing, to receive a copy in a machine-readable format (JSON), and to object to decisions based on automated processing. You can withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your account under Profile → Settings. For other requests, use the contact form or write to info@saltajans.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Changes

When this policy changes we notify users. The current version is always available on this page.
